About

Post-Match Plots is where I put the football questions I can't stop poking at, answered with charts. Mostly Manchester United, sometimes the rest of the league, always with the working shown.

I'm Georgia. I build dashboards and tell stories with data for a living, and this is what I do with it on a Saturday.

How the numbers work

Data comes from FotMob (Opta). Per-90 figures and percentiles compare players with 450+ league minutes in the same position. Early-season numbers move a lot, so every post says how many games it covers.
