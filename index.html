How every post works

One question

Each post asks a single thing, like "Are United unlucky in front of goal?", and sticks to it.

The data, shown

Two or three charts that make the case, built from Opta data via FotMob, with the method at the bottom.

A straight answer

Every post ends with "The answer": yes, no, or "a bit, but not for the reason you think".

About

Post-Match Plots is written by a Manchester United fan who works with data and charts for a living. It's where the questions that nag after a match get answered properly, with the numbers out in the open.

It's mostly United, because that's who I watch. The methods work for anyone.

How the numbers work

Data comes from FotMob (Opta). Per-90 figures and percentiles compare players with 450+ league minutes in the same position. Early-season numbers move a lot, so every post says how many games it covers.
