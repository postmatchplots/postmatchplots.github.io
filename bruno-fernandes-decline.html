Is Bruno Fernandes getting worse?

At creating, yes. At scoring, no. He's making far fewer chances than last season and doing much less without the ball, while shooting more, mostly from distance. But last season's 21 assists were never repeatable, so the drop looks bigger than it is.

Less creating, more shooting

Last season he created about one big chance a game. This season it's under half that. His chances created are down 30% and his xA is down 36%. He's shooting more instead, 3.5 a game against 2.5, and 21 of his roughly 35 shots have come from outside the box, none of them goals. He's also doing a lot less without the ball: his defensive actions have dropped from the 86th percentile to the 32nd.

Last season was too good to last

Before anyone writes him off: last season's 21 assists came from 12.3 xA, which means teammates scored far more of his chances than you'd ever expect. That wasn't going to happen twice. At last season's creation rate he'd have about 3.5 assists by now rather than 2. The drop is real, just not as dramatic as the headline numbers make it look.

The answer

He's a less creative, less active player than a year ago, and more of a shooter. Whether that's a change of role, with Cunha and Mbeumo taking more of the creative load, or simply that he's 32, is something the data can't tell you. Ten games is a short run, so this is one to check again at Christmas.
