So where should United actually be?

About 15th, with 13 points rather than 8. Replay every match from the quality of its chances and United have dropped around five points more than they deserved, the third-biggest shortfall in the league. But 15th is still poor: bad luck explains the gap to the bottom three, not the gap to the top half.

How expected points works

Every shot has an xG: the chance a shot like it goes in. Treat each shot in a match as its own roll of the dice, work out the odds of every possible scoreline, and you get each team's chance of winning, drawing and losing. Three points for each win chance plus one for each draw chance gives the points a performance was "worth". Add them up over 11 games and you get a table of how teams have played, not how the dice landed.

Arsenal are the opposite story: 28 points from chances worth 21, the luckiest side in the league. Forest (-7.4) and Sunderland (-5.7) are the only teams who've been hit harder than United.

Where the points went

Most of United's missing points come from three games. They lost 2-0 at Hull in a match they'd have won 55% of the time on the chances. They lost 1-0 at home to City in what was close to a coin toss. And they drew 4-4 with Villa in a game they were more likely to win than not.

It hasn't all gone against them. The 1-1 with Tottenham was a point they barely deserved: they'd have won that game only 16% of the time.

The rest went roughly as the chances said. United were well beaten at Liverpool, Chelsea and at home to Bournemouth, and no amount of luck changes those.

The answer

United are a 15th-placed team having a 19th-placed season. Some of the gap will close as the finishing evens out, as part 2 showed. But the long shots, the chances they give away and the set pieces are already baked into these numbers, and expected points can't rescue those. Waiting for luck gets United to 15th. Getting higher means fixing the things the rest of this series is about.

How this was made

Data from FotMob (Opta), Premier League 2026/27 up to 24 November 2026. Expected points come from the shot maps of all 110 league matches so far: each team's shots are treated as independent chances with a probability equal to their xG, and every scoreline's odds are worked out exactly. Own goals are left out of expected points. Actual points come from the official table, because a few shot maps are missing a goal. Eleven games is a small sample, so treat every number here as a direction, not a verdict.
