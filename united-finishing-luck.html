Are United unlucky in front of goal?

A little, but less than you'd think. United have scored about four goals fewer than their chances were worth. They're finishing big chances at exactly the league rate, and the shortfall is spread thinly across lots of players rather than one wasteful striker.

Four goals short, which is a lot but not unusual

United's chances were worth 18.2 goals. They've scored 14. Only Forest (-8.7) and Sunderland (-7.8) are clearly further behind, and Fulham are level with United on -4.2.

The big chances are going in

United have had 28 big chances and missed 19, so they've scored 32% of them. The league as a whole scores 32.5%. The clear-cut ones are going in at a perfectly normal rate. The gap is in the harder chances: about 9 of United's 14 goals came from big chances, leaving roughly 5 from everything else.

No single culprit

Look player by player and almost everyone is a little under. The regulars are close to par; it's the players coming off the bench who've been wasteful.

The answer

Yes, slightly. Four goals spread across ten players is the kind of shortfall that usually evens out, and it's worth a few points over a season. But it doesn't explain 19th. The bigger problems are the quality of chances United create and the quality of the ones they give away.
