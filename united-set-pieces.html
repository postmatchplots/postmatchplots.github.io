How much are set pieces costing United?

About six goals. United have scored one set-piece goal and conceded seven, the worst record in the league by a distance. That's two-thirds of their entire goal difference, and the aerial numbers show why.

Worst in the league, by a distance

United have scored one set-piece goal and conceded seven. The next worst teams are at -3, so United are twice as bad as anyone else. Six goals is two-thirds of their whole -9 goal difference.

Their own corners aren't working either: 39 shots from corners, worth 2.0 xG between them, and one goal.

Set-piece goal difference

One man winning headers

Harry Maguire wins 73% of his aerial duels, top 10% for a Premier League centre-back. After him it falls off a cliff. Lisandro Martínez is in the bottom 10% of centre-backs for headers won, and Bryan Mbeumo, Matheus Cunha and Bruno Fernandes all win almost nothing in the air for their positions.

That matters at both ends: no one to aim at when United have a corner, and not enough height when they're defending one.

The answer

Roughly six goals in eleven games, which on its own is the difference between 19th and mid-table. Unlike the finishing, this isn't bad luck: it's a squad short of aerial presence and a set-piece routine that isn't creating anything. It's also the quickest problem to fix, whether with a coach, a centre-back or both.

How this was made

Data from FotMob (Opta), Premier League 2026/27 up to 24 November 2026. Set-piece goals come from FotMob's team leaderboards and match shot maps. Aerial duel figures come from individual FotMob player pages, ranked against players with 450+ league minutes in the same position. Eleven games is a small sample, so treat every number here as a direction, not a verdict.
