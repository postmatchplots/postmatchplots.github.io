Stat glossary

Every stat used on this site, in plain English. The short version: what counts depends on who's doing it. A centre-back with no dribbles and a winger with no clearances are both doing their jobs, so wherever it helps there's a "for scale" line with what's normal for the position.

Shooting

Shots

Any attempt to score: on target, off target, blocked or hitting the woodwork. Own goals aren't shots.

xG (expected goals)

How many goals a player's or team's shots were worth, from the chance each one goes in. Full guide.

npxG

xG with penalties taken out, so whoever takes the penalties doesn't look better than he is.

Big chance

Opta's label for a chance a player would reasonably be expected to score, usually one-on-one or from very close in. A big chance missed is one of those that didn't go in.

Creating

Chances created

Passes that lead straight to a shot, whether or not it goes in. Assists count too. Sometimes called key passes.

For scale: Attacking midfielders and wingers average 1.7 a game, central midfielders 1.2.

xA (expected assists)

The xG of the shots a player sets up. It rewards the pass rather than the finish: a perfect cross that's headed over still earns its xA.

For scale: Attacking midfielders and wingers average 0.16 per 90; Bruno Fernandes is on 0.23.

Big chances created

Passes that set up a big chance (see above).

On the ball

Passes completed

Passes that reach a teammate. Pass accuracy is the share of all attempted passes that do.

For scale: Centre-backs complete the most, about 48 a game at 86%. Strikers complete about 13.

Line-breaking passes

Completed passes that go past a line of opposition players, usually their midfield or defence, to a teammate beyond it. The pass that takes a team up the pitch, not across it. FotMob only shows these on player pages, not in league lists.

Long balls

Completed passes over a long distance, usually from deep.

For scale: Centre-backs complete about 2.4 a game.

Dribbles

Successful take-ons: running at a player and getting past him with the ball. Dribble success is the share of attempts that work.

For scale: Attacking midfielders and wingers manage 1.5 a game; Kobbie Mainoo's 1.8 is the most of any central midfielder.

Without the ball

Tackles

Taking the ball off a player who has it.

For scale: Central midfielders make about 2.1 a game.

Interceptions

Cutting out an opponent's pass.

For scale: Centre-backs make the most, about 1.2 a game.

Clearances

Getting the ball away from danger, usually from your own box, without aiming it at a teammate.

For scale: Centre-backs make about 5.4 a game, central midfielders 1.4.

Blocks

Getting in the way of an opponent's shot.

For scale: Centre-backs block about 0.8 shots a game.

Ball recoveries

Picking up a loose ball that nobody has control of: a second ball, a bad touch, a clearance that drops short. Less glamorous than a tackle, often more important.

For scale: Central midfielders average 4.9; Mainoo's 9.2 is second only to Exequiel Palacios.

Defensive actions

FotMob's "defensive contributions": tackles, interceptions, clearances and blocks added together, plus ball recoveries for midfielders and forwards (not for defenders). One number for how much a player does without the ball.

For scale: Central midfielders average 9.8, centre-backs 9.0, strikers 4.2.

Possession won in the final third

Times a player wins the ball back in the third of the pitch nearest the opposition goal. A rough measure of pressing.

Running

Distance covered

How far a player runs, in kilometres per 90 minutes, from tracking cameras.

For scale: Central midfielders run furthest, about 11.5 km. Centre-backs about 10.3.

Sprints

Runs at top speed, above a set sprint threshold, counted by tracking cameras.

For scale: Full-backs, wingers and strikers make about 13 to 14 a game; centre-backs about 7. Mainoo makes 4.8. Tracking data misses a few players, so where a player's missing, he's left out rather than counted as zero.

Comparing players

Per 90

Any count divided by minutes played, times 90. It lets you compare a player who's played 600 minutes with one who's played 1,000.

Percentile

What share of players in the same position someone is ahead of on a stat. Full guide.

With and without (on/off)

How the team does, usually in xG for and against per 90, with a player on the pitch and with him off it. Interesting, but noisy: it depends on who else was playing and the state of the game. The Mainoo post shows one, with red-card minutes taken out.

Expected points (xPts)

The points a performance was worth, going by the chances. Full guide.

FotMob rating

FotMob's own match rating out of 10, from dozens of stats. Used here only to pick each Monday's star performer.

Definitions follow FotMob and Opta, written in plain English here. All data: FotMob (Opta).
